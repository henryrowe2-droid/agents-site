How I got here

I did not lose deals to better agents. I lost them to my own inbox.

I grew up in Sudbury and I sell real estate in Metro West Massachusetts with The Avenue Team by SERHANT. Luxury and new development, mostly.

A while back I went back through my database and found the same thing every agent finds when they finally look: hundreds of people I had talked to once and never called again. Not because I did not care. Because there were 40 of them and one of me, and the ones who shouted loudest got the attention.

So I started building. First a script that pulled my expired listings every morning. Then something that read my calls and wrote the follow up. Then the thing that audits the whole database and tells me who I forgot.

None of it is theory. It runs on a machine in my office every single day, on real clients, with real commission on the line. When it breaks, it breaks on my business first.

Other agents started asking how. So now I show them.
