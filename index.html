How this started

I was not losing deals to better agents. I was losing them to my own inbox.

I grew up in Sudbury. I sell real estate in Metro West Massachusetts and Boston, luxury and new development mostly, with The Avenue Team by SERHANT.

Like every agent, I had a database I was proud of and quietly afraid of. Hundreds of names. People I met at open houses, referrals from past clients, buyers who called about a listing and were genuinely ready. I knew there was money in there. I also knew I had no idea where.

So one night I actually looked. Not a glance at the dashboard. A real audit, every contact, every call, every note I had ever written.

Nine hundred and nine people had never been called. I had promised things in my own notes that I never delivered. I read them back and did not recognize the person who wrote them.

It was not laziness. There were forty of them and one of me, and the ones who shouted loudest got the attention while the quiet ones went cold. That is the actual math of this business, and no amount of discipline fixes it. You cannot out-hustle arithmetic.

So I started building instead. First a script that pulled every expired listing in my market before I woke up. Then something that listened to my calls and wrote the follow up. Then the audit itself, running on demand, so I would never be surprised by my own database again.

Somewhere in there it stopped being a side project. One morning it caught something I had missed: it read a message thread I had forgotten existed and stopped me from sending a client something I had already sent them weeks earlier. It was not just doing the work. It was catching me.

Other agents started asking how. So now I show them, and I hand over the same systems, in the same shape they run in my own business.

The principle

It does the work. You keep every word.

Nearly every AI product being sold to agents right now wants to talk to your clients for you. Mine will not, and that is not a limitation I am apologizing for. It is the design.

  • It listens to the call and builds the follow up plan with real dates attached
  • It writes the message in your voice, learned from your own writing, not a template
  • It holds the send the moment a client replies, so you are never talking over them
  • It refuses to guess. If your record does not prove it, it will not claim it
