The setup

Six steps, start to finish.

01

Install Claude on your desktop

This is the desktop app, not the website. The browser version cannot hold connectors, and connectors are the entire point of what follows. Go to claude.ai/download, install it for Mac or Windows, and sign in with the account you already have. The free plan finishes this guide.

02

Create your Apify account

Apify is the service that does the actual gathering. Claude is the brain, Apify is the hands. Go to apify.com, sign up, confirm your email, and stop there. You do not need to choose a plan or browse anything yet. Creating the account is free and comes with monthly usage credit.

03

Open Connectors inside Claude

Connectors are how Claude reaches tools that live outside the chat window. In the desktop app, open Settings, then choose Connectors in the sidebar. That screen is where everything in the next step happens.

04

Find Apify and connect it

Search the connector directory for Apify and add it. A browser window opens asking you to sign in to Apify and authorize Claude. Approve it and you are done. If Apify is not listed, choose Add custom connector instead and paste https://mcp.apify.com as the server URL. You will know it worked when Apify shows as connected on that same screen.

05

Link your API token, only if you were not asked to sign in

Most people never touch this step, because the sign in window in step four handles it. Do this only if the connection failed. In Apify, open Settings, then API and Integrations. Your personal token is on that page. Copy it and paste it in when Claude asks. Treat that token exactly like a password: anyone holding it can spend from your account, so it never goes in a screenshot or a shared document.

06

Ask Claude for the list

Start a new chat, paste the prompt below with your own details filled in, and watch it work rather than walking away. Claude will tell you which tool it plans to use before it runs anything. The first list takes a few minutes.

Before you scale it up

Four rules that keep the list honest.

  • Run one category first. Twenty rows, one category, your real towns. Check them by hand before you spend on anything else.
  • Empty beats a guess. A blank email cell costs you nothing. A wrong one gets you marked as spam and burns the introduction permanently.
  • Make it show its work. Ask for the source page beside every claim. If it cannot tell you where a fact came from, the fact is not real.
  • Watch the meter. Runs consume Apify credit. Ask what a run will cost before approving it, and start small enough that a mistake is cheap.

Those rules exist because I have audited a list built without them. Every rating in it was invented, and the invented numbers contradicted the real ones sitting two columns away in the same spreadsheet. A list you cannot trust is worse than no list, because you will call someone with a fact about their own business that is wrong.

One more, and it is the one people skip. These are businesses, not consumers, and the numbers are published. That does not put them outside Do Not Call rules or your brokerage policy. Check before you dial, keep an opt out on every email, and never buy your way around either.
