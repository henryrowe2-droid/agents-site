The shape of the month

Four weeks. Each one earns the next.

The order matters more than any single post. Most people who fail at this fail because they skipped straight to the impressive part before the boring part was in place. So the month is built in the order it actually has to happen, and each week assumes only the week before it.

01

First contact

Small wins with zero setup. Rewriting a listing description you already have. Fixing the voicemail you leave forty times a week. Handling an objection you actually heard on a call, not a made up one from a script book. Nothing this week requires you to install anything, configure anything, or understand anything. Day one assumes you have never opened Claude, and the first win should land in about five minutes.

The two posts I would keep

If everything else got deleted, I would keep these two.

Every other post in the month makes something faster. These two are the ones that keep the speed from costing you your license or your reputation, and almost nobody teaching this publicly spends a full post on either.

Rule

The compliance rules nobody mentions.

Fair housing language does not stop applying because a model wrote the sentence. Do Not Call does not care how the list was built. Opt out has to be real and it has to work. Advertising and record keeping rules still belong to you, with your name on them. This is a full post because it should be, and because the people selling you the shortcut usually leave it out.

Limit

The things I refuse to automate.

Nothing reaches a client without me reading it first. Price conversations, bad news, anything with grief or a divorce or a job loss behind it, and any moment where somebody needs to hear an actual human being. I will show you where I drew those lines and why, including the two I got wrong the first time and had to move back.

Why I am doing this in the open

Nobody buys from a stranger. They buy from someone they watched.

I could have kept all of this quiet and used it. Plenty of agents do, and it is a reasonable choice. Here is the honest reason I am putting it on a public feed instead.

Reason

Most agents will not buy anything until they have watched someone do it for a month.

That is not cynicism, it is just true, and it is true of me too. A single post is a claim. Thirty days in a row is evidence. If you are going to trust anything I build later, you should get to watch me be right and wrong in public first, on my own business, before any money is involved. So here is the month.

Reason

Teaching it forces me to keep it working.

Private systems rot. They break in some small way, you route around the break, and six months later you are doing half of it by hand without noticing. If I have to explain a piece of it out loud to strangers, the broken parts stay visible, and the explanation has to still be true on the day I record it.

Also

I am not pretending this is charity.

It is marketing. It is the kind I would want done to me: give away the useful part, be specific about the limits, and let the person decide. If you follow the whole month and never speak to me again, that is a fine outcome and the month still worked for you.

If you found this on day nineteen

Start on today's post. Go back later, or do not.

Every day stands alone. There is no running plot to catch up on and no numbered homework you missed. Watch whatever is on top today, try that one thing, and let the earlier posts sit there until you want them. The weeks build on each other in theory, and in practice any single post is worth the four minutes on its own.

If today's post lands in week three and you have not done week two, it will still make sense. It will just make more sense after, which is a good reason to scroll back rather than a reason to feel behind.
