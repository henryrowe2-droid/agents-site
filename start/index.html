The setup

Three steps, start to finish.

01

Download the zip and unzip it

Open the receipt, click the download link, save the file, and unzip it. Your Desktop is fine. If you bought more than one pack you will have a link for each, and each one works the same way.

02

Open START-HERE.md and pick your path

That file is the one that matters, and it takes about two minutes. There are two ways to run this pack and both are real. In your browser at claude.ai you paste prompts straight out of PROMPTS.md, with nothing to install and no terminal. On your computer with Claude Code the pack installs itself and the skills trigger on their own without you naming them. START-HERE.md decides which one is yours and walks you through it, including the exact terminal steps if you take the computer route. Most agents should start in the browser. You can move up later, and nothing is lost if you never do.

03

Run voice setup before anything else

On the computer path, say run voice-setup. In the browser, use the voice-setup prompt in PROMPTS.md. Either way it asks about how you actually write: the words you use, the ones you would never say, how long your texts run, how formal you go with a luxury seller against a first time buyer. It wants three to five samples of your real writing and about ten minutes. Every other skill reads that profile before it drafts a line, which is the difference between something that sounds like you and something that sounds like a chatbot in a blazer. Skipping it is the single most common reason a pack ends up sitting unused in a folder.

If something goes wrong

Four things that actually happen.

  • The email has not arrived. Give it five minutes, then search your mail for Payhip and check spam and promotions. If it is genuinely not there, email me and I will send the file directly.
  • Terminal says command not found. Claude Code is not installed on that computer yet. It is free and it runs on the Claude plan you already pay for. START-HERE.md tells you where to get it, or just start in the browser instead and skip the whole thing.
  • Everything it writes sounds generic. You skipped voice setup, or you rushed the answers. Run it again and be honest in the answers, including the parts about what you would never say.
  • You want a human. Reply to your receipt email or DM @henryhandleshomes on Instagram. Both reach me, not a help desk and not a ticket queue.
